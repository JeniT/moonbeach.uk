# Artwork used on the site

All artwork on the site is public domain. The originals are in `full/`; the
versions in `public/art/` were re-printed in the site's four inks
(#24180f, #7c3522, #cf8f3a, #f0e2c4) by `inks.py` and `ink_print.py`
(Python with Pillow; only needed if the images are ever re-made).

| Used for | Work | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Home page and inner page headers (`drawbridge-*.webp`) | Giovanni Battista Piranesi, *The Drawbridge*, Carceri d'Invenzione plate VII, c.1750 (d. 1778) | Wikimedia Commons, Google Art Project scan, marked public domain |
| Worlds page (`wheel-*.webp`) | Giovanni Battista Piranesi, *The Giant Wheel*, Carceri d'Invenzione plate IX, c.1750 | Wikimedia Commons, The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access (DP828183) |
| The Eldritch Earl (`eldritch-earl-castle.webp`) | William T. Horton (1864–1919), castle illustration | Taken from the Eldritch Earl player's guide, which credits it as public domain |
| The Eldritch Earl map (`eldritch-earl-map.jpg`) | Map of Cawthorne and western Nancarrow | Made by Bill Beacham with Wonderdraft; from the player's guide |
| The Shrine of Khaos (`shrine-of-khaos.webp`) | Wallace Smith (1888–1937), signed line illustration | Supplied by Bill as the adventure's cover; public domain. Original in `full/shrine-of-khaos.jpg` |
| Only Angels Have Wings (`only-angels-have-wings.webp`) | William T. Horton (1864–1919), signed line illustration of an angel | Supplied by Bill as the adventure's cover; public domain. Original in `full/only-angels-have-wings.jpg` |

The other small images in this folder (Doré, Kauffer and more Piranesi) are
the alternatives that were considered while choosing the design. They are
not used on the site. E. McKnight Kauffer died in 1954, so his work became
public domain in the UK in 2025.
