(With thanks to Erin B for that choice phrase). 

OK so the title is obviously tongue-in-cheek, but still...

Lazy mornings in hazy sunshine, choice music on the diegetic soundtrack, ill-advised use of drugs and alcohol, and favourite characters taking bullets to the stomach… the similarities between the two franchises are uncanny :)

While in the past you couldn’t accuse GTA of being anything like PC, and there’s still plenty of content that wouldn’t look out of place in *Gen V* (my personal benchmark for “WTF have I just seen on my TV screen?”), the focus in Chapter 1 on slower gameplay, on building a meaningful relationship based on actually listening to your partner and thinking about what they might like or dislike, and actually playing as a female character, all feel so refreshing within this context. In part due to this, and in part because the world they have built just feels so incredibly realistic, I found myself NOT committing crimes in case it got us in trouble with Lucia’s PO. (Well, apart from undertaking slow-moving cars and jumping red lights, I’m only human after all.)

Totally not what I was expecting from this game.  

Chapter 2 is going more old-school GTA for me, and loving that too.

Oh, and congratulations to Manni L. Perez for her Game Awards nomination for Best Performance, definitely well deserved, if only for Lucia’s laugh.
