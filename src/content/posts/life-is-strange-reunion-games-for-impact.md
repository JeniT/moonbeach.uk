Wonderful (if inevitably bittersweet) to see that *Life is Strange: Reunion* among the nominees for this year’s Game Awards ‘Games for Impact’. From the very first game, Life is Strange has always been about challenging conceptions about what content is ‘right’ for video games, and has been unafraid to champion causes and messages that can make a difference. 

Working on these games alongside the rest of the immensely talented and dedicated teams at Don’tNod, Deck Nine, at Square Enix has been one of the proudest and most important parts of my career. 

There are other great games up for the award - *Big Walk*, *Cairn*, *Forever Ago*, *lily’s world XD* and *Mixtape* – and it’s a credit to everyone involved in showing that games can be truly meaningful. Looking forward to Dec 10th when the winners will be announced.
