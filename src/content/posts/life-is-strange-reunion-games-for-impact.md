Lovely news: *Life is Strange: Reunion* is one of six nominees for Games for Impact at this year's Game Awards, the category for games with a meaningful pro-social message. It's in good company, alongside *Big Walk*, *Cairn*, *Forever Ago*, *lily's world XD* and *Mixtape*.

Championing *Life is Strange*, and seeing how much it has meant to so many players, has been one of the proudest parts of my career. Congratulations to Deck Nine and everyone who worked on the final chapter of Max and Chloe's story. The winners are announced on 10 December.
