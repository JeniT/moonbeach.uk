*Grand Theft Auto VI* is out today, thirteen years after *GTA V*. Pre-orders alone are estimated at over $1.6 billion, roughly double GTA V's record-breaking launch, and it picked up eight Game Awards nominations before anyone outside Rockstar had played it.

It's worth remembering how much of an outlier that is. Almost every lesson I've learned about scope, schedules and doing the essentials brilliantly assumes a team has to ship within normal limits of time and money. GTA plays by different rules, and very few games, or studios, can. The rest of us should enjoy it, and be careful about drawing conclusions from it.
