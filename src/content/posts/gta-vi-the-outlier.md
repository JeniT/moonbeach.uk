title: "GTA VI: the outlier"
date: 2026-11-19

---

*Grand Theft Auto VI* is out today, thirteen years after the last one. It has been inescapable. Pre-orders alone are estimated at over $1.6 billion, roughly double GTA V’s record-breaking launch, and it already picked up eight Game Awards nominations.

It’s worth remembering how much of an outlier that is. Working on *Just Cause*, we had to benchmark against other open world games, and the GTA series would always throw those numbers out of whack if we let them. We talked about the difference between AAA and AAAA games, but GTA is in a class of its own in every regard: team size, development time, expenditure, and (justifiable) grand expectations. 

GTA plays by different rules than anything else out there, now more than ever. The rest of us should enjoy it, and be extremely wary about drawing conclusions from it when talking about what it means for the videogame industry as a whole. As with Larian and *Baldur’s Gate 3*, it took decades of investment and learning (in a very different environment to where the industry is today) for Rockstar get to where they are today, and there are very few studios currently in existence who could hope to creatively and financially emulate them. 

People should be ambitious, for sure, but try not to get lost in the hype about all those record-breaking numbers.
