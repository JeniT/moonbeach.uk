*The Eldritch Earl* is a campaign set in the Northern Borderlands, a gritty and superstitious feudal region where rival rulers – the Baron of Cawthorne and the Earl of Nancarrow – vie for power.

It is written for fifth edition D&D, but with an old-school sensibility about freedom of choice and the need to be smart about tackling dangerous situations. Not every encounter is "fair" or balanced if you resort to violence every time. Negotiation, stealth, trickery and misdirection are all valuable survival techniques.

The adventure offers freedom not only in how to approach individual problems, but also in your characters' motivations and what you will consider to be victory. You might be righteous crusaders for justice, convicts desperate to avoid imprisonment (or worse), or self-serving opportunists looking to seize the Barony from the man who is fool enough to think you are there to save him.

The tone and setting are inspired by the classic British roleplaying games of the 1980s, such as first edition *Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay* and *Dragon Warriors*. As a result, the Borderlands are less overtly fantastical than some settings: non-humans are uncommon, and magic users (apart from those of the divine) tend to be treated with suspicion or hostility.

## The story so far

A month ago, the Baron's heirloom blade was stolen from the family vault beneath Caerhays Keep. Three weeks ago, his only son and heir, Felix, was struck down by elf-shot while hunting in the Forest of Shadows, and still lies in an unbreakable sleep. Undead soldiers have marched south to raid the Barony, and the Drowners grow bolder every night.

A week ago, assailed on every side and stricken by the fall of his son, Baron Rinduran Cawthorne sent out a call for help from any possible quarter, and your characters have answered. Now play on to find out what happens next.
